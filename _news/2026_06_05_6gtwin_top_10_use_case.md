Two projects in which I am deeply involved, 6G-TWIN and AMBIENT-6G, participated at EuCNC & 6G Summit 2026 in Malaga. It is especially rewarding to receive the news that the Energy Savings use case of 6G-TWIN, where imec leads the efforts, was announced among 10 winners of the SNS JU Use Case Contest. Our use case was selected from an intial portfolio of 469 JU SNS use cases. Congratulations to the whole 6G-TWIN consortium!
